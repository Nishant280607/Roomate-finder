# RoomieFinder

Find a flatmate you'll actually get along with.

RoomieFinder matches people looking for a place to share by the things that matter day to day: budget, area, move-in date, sleep, tidiness, guests, noise, smoking, pets and food.

## What you can do

- Sign in with Google or with your email
- Set up your profile in a few short steps
- Browse people in your city, each with a match score and the reasons you'd get along
- Send a connection request with a short note
- Chat once you've both said yes
- Edit your preferences and share them as a card in any chat
- Save people to come back to later
- Choose light or dark mode
